Urgent means expiring

An item is urgent when its estimated expiry falls inside the unilateral exit window — the period in which you should renew it or prepare an on-chain exit. Estimates are not guarantees.

Unknown never means safe

If expiry evidence is missing or the snapshot is stale, the item is marked unknown and kept near the top — never silently treated as healthy. Verify the source before relaxing.

Local only, by design

Your watchlist lives only in this browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere, and this desk cannot notify you — set your own reminders and check back yourself.
