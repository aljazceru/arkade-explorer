Sources

Where each number on this page would come from, and what it cannot tell you.

A

Arkade

Operator parameters from /v1/info and transactions from the Arkade indexer. In production, time to expiry is computed from absolute expiry and the current chain tip, never from a stored countdown.

S

Second

Second does not publish a VTXO-by-address index. Records here are synthetic examples; public on-chain activity is the only independent evidence available.

What “needs review” means

Expiry falls inside the network’s exit window, or there is no expiry evidence at all. Unknown never counts as safe.

Virtual vs. commitment

Virtual transactions live in the protocol; commitments are Bitcoin transactions anchoring a batch. A confirmed commitment does not prove a virtual output is spendable.
